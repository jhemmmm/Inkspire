## Phase Goal

**As a** Frontline Staff member, **I want to** have each job order I create automatically validated (when it needs a print-ready file) or automatically routed to an available Artist (when it needs consultation), **so that** every job order becomes a real, production-ready record without any manual validation or assignment step.

<objective>
Wire the outcomes Plan 03-01's backend already computes and exposes onto the two screens Frontline Staff actually uses: the New Visit confirmation card (the primary surface, since D-03 makes the outcome available in the very same response that renders the card) and the Queue table (the durable, return-visit surface for a `validation_failed` row a staff member navigates back to later). No new page, nav item, or portal is introduced — this is purely the frontend half of Phase 3's one vertical slice, completing what Plan 03-01 left render-ready.

Purpose: JOB-01/JOB-02 are only genuinely "done" once a real Frontline Staff user can see the outcome and act on a failure without any backend knowledge — a status badge with no visual language, or a failure reason with no way to fix it, is not a complete slice.
Output: A new shared `ReplaceJobOrderFileDialog.vue` component, plus the status-badge/Alert/artist-name/Replace-File wiring in both `NewVisit.vue` and `QueueList.vue`, exactly per `03-UI-SPEC.md`'s Color, Copywriting, and Phase-Specific UI Notes sections.
</objective>
