## Phase Goal

**As an** Artist, **I want to** take a Type B job order from consultation through a locked, approved design, **so that** the job order becomes ready for pricing at the Cashier and production never depends on an unfinished or unapproved design.

<objective>
Build the backend for the first two requirements an Artist touches after Phase 3 auto-assigns a Type B job order: recording consultation notes on that same job order (JOB-03, D-01/D-02 — no new job-order-creation flow), and the Artist's own consultation-queue controls, Next/Forward/Not-Appear (JOB-09, D-03/D-04). This plan introduces the `InConsultation` status, two new columns for queue ordering/deprioritization, and two new Artist-scoped controllers. It is deliberately backend-only — Plan 04-02 wires the Vue Dashboard and the new Job Order Workspace page on top of the exact endpoints and props this plan ships.

Purpose: Every later slice in this phase (design editor, review, session status, performance report) either extends the `JobOrderStatus` enum this plan introduces or renders inside the Job Order Workspace page this plan's sibling frontend plan creates. Getting the status machine and ownership guards right here, with dedicated Pest coverage, is the foundation the rest of the phase builds on without re-litigating.
Output: One additive migration, one new `JobOrderStatus` case, two new Artist controllers (`JobOrderQueueController`, `JobOrderWorkspaceController`), two Form Requests, route wiring under the existing `artist.` group, and full Pest coverage for consultation notes and all three queue-control transitions including their ownership/ordering guards.
</objective>
