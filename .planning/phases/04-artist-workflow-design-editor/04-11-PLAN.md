## Phase Goal

**As an** Artist, **I want to** take a Type B job order from consultation through a locked, approved design, **so that** the job order becomes ready for pricing at the Cashier and production never depends on an unfinished or unapproved design.

<objective>
Add a second, unauthenticated way for the client to record the same design verdict the Artist already records in person: an emailed, signed link (D-17 through D-21). A design entering `pending_review` now also emails the client a 7-day `URL::temporarySignedRoute()` link (via Resend, D-18) to a public page showing the flattened design, the job order description, and Approve / Request Changes buttons. Clicking either reaches the exact same two outcomes `DesignEditorController::approve()`/`requestChanges()` already produce — through entirely independent, new code, since that controller and its guards stay byte-for-byte unchanged (D-17). First verdict wins (D-20): a link that's already been resolved (in person or remotely) or superseded by a newer revision renders a graceful state instead of acting on stale data or crashing.

Purpose: This is user-forced, acknowledged scope creep beyond Phase 4's original artist-only ROADMAP goal (see 04-CONTEXT.md's "2026-09-03 expansion" note) — the user explicitly chose to fold it into Phase 4 rather than open a new phase. Keeping `DesignEditorController` untouched is the load-bearing constraint that makes this safe: the already-shipped, already-tested in-person review cycle (04-03/04-05) cannot regress from this addition.
Output: `App\Mail\DesignReviewRequested`, a mail trigger wired into `RecordDesignRevision`, three new unauthenticated signed routes, `App\Http\Controllers\Public\DesignReviewController`, `resources/js/pages/public/DesignReview.vue`, and a friendly expired-link handler in `bootstrap/app.php`.
</objective>
