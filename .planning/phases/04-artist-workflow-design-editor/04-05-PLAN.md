## Phase Goal

**As an** Artist, **I want to** take a Type B job order from consultation through a locked, approved design, **so that** the job order becomes ready for pricing at the Cashier and production never depends on an unfinished or unapproved design.

<objective>
Close the design review cycle Plan 04-03 opened: the Artist records the client's in-person verdict (D-05 — no separate reviewer role or customer portal), approval locks the design file, and only an Owner can reverse that lock via an audited override (JOB-07). This plan is backend-only; Plan 04-06 wires the Vue Review section and the new Owner Design Overrides page on top of it.

Purpose: JOB-07's "written to the audit trail" requirement is satisfied for free as long as the unlock is a plain Eloquent mutation on the already-`#[ObservedBy(AuditObserver::class)]`-tagged `DesignFile` model — this plan deliberately does not hand-roll a second, manual audit call (04-RESEARCH.md's Don't Hand-Roll table).
Output: `DesignFilePolicy`, the `approve`/`requestChanges` verdict endpoints, the Owner-only `unlock` endpoint and its Design Overrides list query, and full Pest coverage for both.
</objective>
