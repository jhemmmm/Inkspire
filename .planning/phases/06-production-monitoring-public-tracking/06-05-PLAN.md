Give Frontline Staff a live view of every job order waiting to be picked up (PROD-03), landing on their Dashboard the moment they log in, and surface a lightweight banner on the Queue page they already work from. Purpose: PROD-03's "Frontline Staff receives an alert" becomes literal — the list is in front of them on arrival, with no navigation required, and the existing "Release to Customer" action (built in Phase 5) gets a second, more visible entry point.
Output: A real `frontline-staff/Dashboard.vue` (replacing the placeholder), a ready-for-pickup banner on `QueueList.vue`, and the backend queries both poll.
</objective>
