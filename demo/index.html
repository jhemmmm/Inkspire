Unpaid Cancellation Fee Behavior
If a client refuses to pay the cancellation fee on the spot, the system logs an AR entry tagged "Cancellation Fee — Unpaid". The client record is flagged with an outstanding balance. Frontline Staff will see this flag when the client returns — a new order cannot be processed until the balance is resolved or waived by Owner/Admin.
What Counts as "Design Work Started"?
The system detects design activity (file creation, template selection, or editor interaction) on the JO before cancellation. If no design activity is recorded, the cancellation is fee-free regardless of how long the client was in consultation.
